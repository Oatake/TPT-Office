1. **3 Subtraction Mistakes to Watch For** (2nd grade edition)
2. **Mistake 1: Digits in the wrong place.** 56 − 3 is not 26. The 3 means 3 ones, so it goes under the 6. 56 − 3 = 53.
3. **Mistake 2: Taking the smaller digit from the bigger one.** 63 − 28 is not 45. There are not enough ones to take away 8, so trade a ten: 13 − 8 = 5, 5 − 2 = 3. 63 − 28 = 35.
4. **Mistake 3: A fact slip after a good trade.** 54 − 17: the trade is right (4 tens, 14 ones), but 14 − 7 = 7, not 8. 54 − 17 = 37.
5. **One question helps:** Look at the ones first. "Are there enough ones to take away?" Regroup or not?
