Do your students subtract before they look at the ones? This 2 digit subtraction with and without regrouping set teaches 2nd graders to check the ones, decide "Regroup or not?", and then solve. Three differentiated levels, a cut-and-paste sort, error analysis, word problems, and exit tickets come in one 27-page printable PDF with answer keys.

Students join the Regroup Crew: every page is a job on the build site. They check the ones before each job, trade a ten when they need more ones, and inspect the work when they finish.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (27-page PDF)
• Teacher pages (2): How to Use guide with a suggested 7-day sequence, plus a What's Inside map that shows each page's level, regroup type, standards, and answer key page
• Crew Card student helper (1 page, 2 copies): a step-by-step "Regroup or Not?" reference with a base-ten worked example, sized for math notebooks

○ Level 1: No Regrouping (2 pages)
• Ones First, Then Tens: 12 problems with T/O place-value boxes
• Smooth Build: 16 problems, no scaffold

□ Level 2: Regrouping (2 pages)
• Trade a Ten!: draw base-ten blocks and solve (worked example + 5 problems)
• Trade-a-Ten Workshop: 12 problems; the regroup boxes fade out after problem 6

△ Level 3: Mixed, Regroup or Not? (9 pages)
• Check the Ones: circle YES or NO, then solve (12 problems)
• Crew Sort: cut-and-paste sort with 10 cards and a sort mat (2 pages)
• Measure Twice: subtract, then check by adding (8 problems)
• Mystery Build: Solve & Shade (12 problems + hidden-picture grid). The grid includes common wrong answers, so a mistake shows up in the picture.
• Site Inspector: Spot the Mistake: 4 student work samples to check and fix (one is correct)
• Inspector's Report: Explain It: 3 problems, including a base-ten "prove it"
• Work Orders: 2 pages of word problems (8 problems: one- and two-step, with result, change, and start unknowns)

☆ Challenge (2 pages)
• Big Build: 3-digit Regroup or Not? (12 problems, at most one trade each)
• Blueprint Puzzle: Missing Digits (8 problems)

• Crew Time Cards: 4 quarter-page exit tickets (one for each level, plus a word problem)
• Answer keys (6 pages), with sample explanations and the finished mystery picture
• Terms of Use & Credits (1 page)

HOW TO USE
• Whole group: teach the "look at the ones first" routine with the Crew Card, then practice on the Level 3 pages.
• Small groups and intervention: the shape in each page corner (○ □ △ ☆) helps you group students without labels like "easy" or "hard." Shape meanings are on the teacher page only.
• Math centers: laminate the sort cards and mat for a reusable center.
• Exit tickets: use the four tickets to form tomorrow's groups.
• Early finishers: the challenge pages extend to 3-digit subtraction and missing-digit puzzles.
• 3rd grade: use as review or intervention.

Prep is printing. Print one-sided so the sort cards and sort mat are on separate sheets. No scissors? Students can write R or N next to each card and copy the problems onto the mat. Every page prints in black and white.

Student pages use the words "regroup" and "trade a ten." The word "borrow" is not used. Base-ten drawings are welcome on every page, and the teacher guide explains why the standard algorithm is not required in 2nd grade.

STANDARDS (Common Core)
• 2.NBT.B.5: fluently add and subtract within 100 using place value strategies (main focus)
• 2.NBT.B.9: explain why addition and subtraction strategies work (Spot the Mistake, Explain It, Missing Digits)
• 2.OA.A.1: one- and two-step word problems within 100 (Word Problems, Exit Ticket D)
• 2.NBT.B.7: subtract within 1000 (3-digit challenge page)
Full standard text is printed on page 3.

GRADE LEVEL
2nd grade. Also useful for 3rd grade review and intervention.

FORMAT
Printable PDF, US Letter (8.5 x 11 in). This is not an editable or digital version.

TERMS OF USE
Single classroom / single family use. Do not share, resell, or post publicly online. Sharing with colleagues? Please buy additional licenses.
