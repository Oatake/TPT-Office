Description

Do your 1st graders stop reading when a word gets longer? This low-prep set teaches two-syllable words with one simple routine: find the vowels, split it, read each part, and blast off! Students practice compound words (sun | set) and closed-syllable VC/CV words (nap | kin) with worksheets, a maze, riddles, a decodable mini-book, and 2 Race to the Moon board games, all in one 24-page printable PDF with answer keys.

In Word Rockets, every two-syllable word is a two-stage rocket. Students find where the rocket splits, read each stage, and then blast off by reading the whole word. Tinbot, a friendly robot co-pilot, appears on the anchor chart, in the maze, and in the mini-book.

PREP

Mostly no-prep printables + low-prep games (just add a paper clip). 9 of the 15 student pages need nothing but printing. The rest need a little prep:

Race to the Moon boards: a paper clip and a pencil for the spinner, plus game pieces (cut 2 rockets from the Flight Log, or use erasers or coins)

Docking Station and Engine Count: scissors and glue (no-scissors options are in the Teacher Prep Guide)

Mini-book: one sheet that you fold and cut once (or cut the 8 pages apart and staple)

Terms of Use & Credits

HOW TO USE

Whole group: teach the routine with the Mission Briefing anchor chart, then model one page together.

Small groups and intervention: a small moon, planet, or comet icon on each page shows the level. Students never see the words "easy" or "hard."

Literacy centers: one game of Race to the Moon takes about 10 to 15 minutes. Print the boards on cardstock or slide them into sheet protectors to reuse them.

Fluency: use Countdown Reading and the mini-book for repeated reading.

Quick check: Mission Check items are grouped by level, so you can see which skill each student needs to practice next.

2nd grade: use the Comet pages and the Mission Check for review.

PHONICS NOTES

Every target word uses closed syllables with short vowels: compound words made of two closed-syllable words (bath | tub), and VC/CV words (also called VCCV) with twin consonants (kit | ten), two different consonants (nap | kin), or a blend (bas | ket). Words that do not follow this pattern, such as rocket, robot, and planet, appear only in the pictures and the teacher notes. Student text uses Andika, a font with a single-story a and g.

STANDARDS (Common Core)

RF.1.3.e: Decode two-syllable words following basic patterns by breaking the words into syllables. (main focus)

RF.1.3.d: Use knowledge that every syllable must have a vowel sound to determine the number of syllables in a printed word.

RF.1.3.b: Decode regularly spelled one-syllable words. (supporting: the parts of each compound word)

RF.1.4.b: Read grade-level text orally with accuracy, appropriate rate, and expression on successive readings. (supporting: Countdown Reading and the mini-book)

Full standard text is printed on page 3.
